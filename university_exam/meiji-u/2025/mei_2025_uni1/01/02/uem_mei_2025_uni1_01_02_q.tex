    正の実数からなる数列 $\{a_n\}$ が
    \begingroup
        \setlength{\abovedisplayskip}{4pt}
        \setlength{\belowdisplayskip}{3.2pt}
        \setlength{\jot}{0pt}
        \begin{align*}
            & a_1 = 1,\quad a_2 = 9, \\
            & 9{a_{n+1}}^2 - a_na_{n+2} = 0 \quad (n=1,2,3,\cdots\cdots)
        \end{align*}
    \endgroup
    を満たすとする．$a_n>0$ であることに注意して
    \begingroup
        \setlength{\abovedisplayskip}{8pt}
        \setlength{\belowdisplayskip}{4pt}
        \setlength{\jot}{0pt}
        \begin{align*}
            b_n = \frac{\,a_{n+1}\,}{a_n} \quad (n=1,2,3,\cdots\cdots)
        \end{align*}
    \endgroup
    とおく．このとき
    \begingroup
        \setlength{\abovedisplayskip}{8pt}
        \setlength{\belowdisplayskip}{4pt}
        \setlength{\jot}{0pt}
        \begin{align*}
            b_n =\:\text{\myboxb{　イ　}}\:^{\raisebox{4pt}{$\scriptstyle n$}}
        \end{align*}
    \endgroup
    となるので
    \begingroup
        \setlength{\abovedisplayskip}{4pt}
        \setlength{\belowdisplayskip}{4pt}
        \setlength{\jot}{0pt}
        \begin{align*}
            a_n =\:\text{\myboxb{　ウ　}}\:%
            ^{\raisebox{4pt}{$\scriptstyle n(n-\:\text{\normalsize{\myboxb{　エ　}}}\:)$}}
        \end{align*}
    \endgroup
    である．
